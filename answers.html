Common questions

Is a dry-out quote the same as a rebuild quote?

No. Extraction and structural drying are mitigation. Demo and new finishes are reconstruction. Ask for each in writing.

Why do Dallas dry-out quotes jump from a few thousand to ten thousand or more?

One quote may be fans and dehumidifiers for a few days. Another adds cavity demo, Category 3 work, contents, and rebuild. Ask what is drying only.

Who stops the leak?

Ask whether a plumber or the restoration crew stops the source, and which invoice that work sits on.

Should rebuild start before the structure is dry?

Ask for moisture readings and a written dry standard before new drywall or flooring goes in.
