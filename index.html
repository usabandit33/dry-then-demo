Water in the house. Get the dry-out in writing before anyone starts demo or rebuild.

Dallas homeowners mix a fan-and-dehumidifier bill with demolition and reconstruction. One r/Dallas thread saw a $10–12k Servpro dry-out quote on ceiling damage while another shop priced similar work far lower. Use this list before you sign one bundled number.

  • Ask for a written dry-out scope: rooms, equipment, days, and moisture readings.
  • Ask which materials they plan to demo versus dry in place, and why.
  • Ask whether rebuild (drywall, paint, flooring) is a separate quote after the structure is dry.
  • Ask how they document the loss for insurance without treating coverage as a promise.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
